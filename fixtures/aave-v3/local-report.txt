Assignment: Aave V3 Lending Research Verification.

Supply and borrowing: Users supply assets to a liquidity pool. Assets enabled as collateral can support borrowing within the market's risk limits. Adequate collateral must be maintained. Aave's borrowing guide supports this explanation; Chainlink's separate money-market overview corroborates the pooled, collateral-backed model.

Health factor and liquidation: Aave describes health factor as liquidation-threshold-adjusted collateral value divided by borrowed value. A value below 1 makes a position eligible for liquidation. Collateral price changes and growing debt can weaken it. Liquidators can repay debt and receive collateral under protocol rules. A health factor above 1 does not remove market or smart-contract risk.

Variable borrowing interest: Aave borrowing rates respond to utilization and governance parameters. More borrowing relative to supplied liquidity can make funds scarcer; repayments or added supply change that balance. Variable rates can change over time, so debt accrues interest at a changing rate rather than a promised fixed return. Chainlink independently explains the general supply-and-demand rate mechanism.
